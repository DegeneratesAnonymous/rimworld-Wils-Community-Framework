# Wil's Role Modules : Agriculture

The Agriculture module turns plant work, hunting and animal handling into three related professional paths. Foragers, Trackers and Handlers are entry roles. They qualify into Farmer, Hunter and Rancher, then specialize.

## Career tree

| Entry role | Level two | Specialties |
|---|---|---|
| Forager | Farmer | Horticulturist, Herbalist, Arborist |
| Tracker | Hunter | Stalker, Gamekeeper, Big-Game Hunter |
| Handler | Rancher | Shepherd, Breeder, Beastmaster |

Qualifications recognize experience and skills. The player still chooses the active assignment. Specialty benefits require both qualification and assignment.

## Automatic hunting

The Community window's Agriculture page stores a colony hunting policy. Automatic hunting is opt-in. It adds ordinary vanilla hunt designations and records exactly which designations it created. When the meat target is reached, a target becomes invalid, or automatic hunting is disabled, it removes only those recorded designations. A hunt designated by the player is never added to that record and is never restricted or removed by this system.

A Tracker may carry out any hunt the player designates. Tracker-level automatic selection is conservative: small, nearby, low-revenge game. A Hunter unlocks policy-driven small and medium game. Assigned Stalkers extend the automatic search range, Gamekeepers favor sustainable populations, and Big-Game Hunters unlock large or predatory automatic targets when the player enables those policy choices.

The first gameplay pass must validate animal classification, meat counting across modded foods, designation cleanup, target reachability, performance on large maps, and the balance of progression and bonuses.

## Events

Every entry, established and specialist role has five positive mood events, five negative mood events and five relationship events. Successful plant, hunting and livestock work triggers the first positive production stories. Social interactions between agriculture workers can trigger the relationship set. Situational negative triggers such as spoiled harvests, lost quarry, animal injuries and overhunting remain the next narrative integration pass; their catalog and thoughts are already defined.
